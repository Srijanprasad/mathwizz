### Full Description (max 4000 characters)
```
Sharpen your mind and calculate with confidence using MathSprint! 

MathSprint is a fast-paced, beautifully designed mental arithmetic game created for students, casual players, and adults who want to master mental mathematics. Whether you have 2 minutes on your commute or want a focused daily workout, MathSprint delivers immediate, rewarding mathematical challenges tailored to your skill level.

⚡ 5 EXCITING GAME MODES:
• Quick Play: Jump into a fast 10-question sprint across mixed operations with zero setup.
• Daily Challenge: A fresh, consistent puzzle set refreshed every midnight. Compare your score with friends offline!
• Topic Practice: Deep-dive into specific areas: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages.
• Time Attack: Race against a 60-second countdown to build massive combo streaks and beat your high score.
• Level Journey: Progress from Novice Runner to Grandmaster Solver across progressive skill bands.

🧠 ROBUST & FAIR MATHEMATICS:
• Clean integer division (no messy rounding or divide-by-zero).
• Subtraction calibrated for positive mental math practice.
• Exact fraction and decimal calculations with clear step-by-step mistake reviews.
• Plausible distractors that test true calculation understanding, not trickery.

🔒 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE-FIRST:
• No account or login required.
• Works completely offline with zero internet dependency.
• Zero data collection, zero trackers, and zero third-party ads.
• All high scores, badges, and progress remain securely on your device.

✨ DESIGNED FOR ACCESSIBILITY & SPEED:
• High-contrast Material 3 design with thumb-friendly touch targets.
• Dual visual feedback (distinct icons and colors) so color alone is never required.
• Support for system font scaling and optional Reduced Motion for sensitive players.
• Dynamic dark and light themes.

Download MathSprint today and transform mental arithmetic into your daily superpower!
```
